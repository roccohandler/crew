## §0 · THE TWO COMPLAINTS, AND WHAT THEY ACTUALLY ARE

The owner said: *"it looks too complicated and things don't look like they're syncing."*

Four Home passes have now treated that first clause as a content problem. It is not.

### Complaint 1 — "too complicated" is a TREATMENT problem, not a fact problem

The measured driver of a "this looks busy" verdict is the number of distinct visual treatments and the
quality of the grid — not the number of facts or the number of actions. Crew's Home fails on three counts
that can be counted:

| Measure | Home today | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct `.font()` expressions in `ios/Crew/Features/Home/` | **13**, across 9 semantic sizes | ≤ 4 |
| Typography tokens in `shared/design-tokens.json` | **zero — the key does not exist** | 5–6 roles |
| Surface/boundary treatments on one screen | 4 | 1–2 |
| Corner radii in use app-wide | 5 | 2–3 |

`shared/design-tokens.json` carries `colors`, `spacing`, `size`, `opacity`, `motion` and `haptics`. It has
**no typography key at all**, so every font size in the app is a per-file decision, and web sets its sizes
inline in CSS with no parity test — the two engines can drift on type silently and forever. This is the
single highest-leverage change available and it is invisible in a diff review, which is why four consecutive
Home passes have walked past it.

On top of that, Home says the same thing two and three times:

- The **state name renders twice, verbatim**, on three of four non-bridge states — `"Plan paused"` as the
  nav title (`HomeScreen.swift:140`) ~250 pt above `"Plan paused"` as the card headline (`TodayCard.swift:87`).
  Same for `"Done for today"` / `"Done for today."`.
- The **week is encoded three ways** stacked: the ring fraction + its caption, seven marks, and the sentence
  `"This week: Wed done · next Fri"` — ~137 pt of column for one fact.
- **Today's workout is reachable from three controls at three weights.** `HomeScreen.swift:98`'s handler is
  byte-identical to `HomeScreen.swift:85`'s. With a session open, `HomeScreen.swift:62` renders
  `"Resume workout · Push day"` while `TodayCard.swift:67` renders `"Resume workout"` — one verb, two
  weights, one destination.

And **nothing has a stable y**. `HomeScreen.swift:79` puts `Spacer(minLength: 0)` *above* the card, so the
card's position is a function of the total height of everything above it — and eleven elements toggle
presence between states (camera, ring, streak caption, week strip, shield line, next-up block, resume
banner, offline banner, Quick complete, crew strip, and the card's own bottom control, which changes
identity, fill AND height: 56 pt ink → 44 pt outline → nothing). Earning a shield moves every control below it.

### Complaint 2 — "doesn't look like it's syncing" is LITERALLY TRUE. Four bugs.

No layout change fixes any of these.

| # | Bug | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** | `loadState` — which gates the offline banner and the error state — is assigned in exactly **one place** (`HomeScreen.swift:161`, inside `load()`), which runs only on `.task` and on `scenePhase → .active`. But **eight** paths call `model.refresh()` directly (session + cardio completions, celebration + nutrition sheet dismissals, `endPause`, `startBonus`, `quickComplete`, the edge prompts). So after you log anything, the banner/error layer is whatever it was at the last foreground. | `HomeScreen.swift:37-45,161` |
| **S2** | `LocalPause` has **one writer in the entire app** — `SettingsModel.pause(until:)`. `ServerHydrate` pulls no pauses at all. Pause on web, or reinstall while paused, and **Settings says "Plan paused" while Home offers Start workout** and stamps `isPlannedDay: true`. | `ServerHydrate.swift` (no pause code) |
| **S3** | `HomeModel` samples `SyncQueue.shared.offline` at `refresh()` time (`HomeModel.swift:101`) — **before** the async drain that would set it — and `SyncQueue` is not `@Observable`, so the flag is stale in both directions and sticky. | `HomeModel.swift:101` |
| **S4** | Home's cardio row filters `workoutKind == "cardio"` (`HomeModel+Facts.swift:92`), while Progress counts cardio sets from **every** session. Cardio done inside a push day **counts on Progress and not on Home**. | `HomeModel+Facts.swift:92` |

Plus: **Home has no delivery signal at all.** The only "Sending ↻" affordance in the app lives in the
Progress tab. And the one element whose entire job is to explain a lag — the offline banner — is a
`#FFFFFF` strip on a `#FAF8F5` canvas behind a 1.19:1 edge, with no icon. It is invisible even when it renders.

### What the owner was actually comparing

**The mockup's dark palette is not a new design — it is Crew's existing dark theme.** Verified by hand:
`shared/design-tokens.json` carries a `light` AND a `dark` hex for all 16 colour tokens;
`EmberColors.swift:47-52` builds each as a `UIColor { traits in ... }` dynamic provider; `ember.css:69`
carries the matching `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block; `ios/project.yml` sets
`UIUserInterfaceStyle: Automatic`. Crew's dark canvas `#171412` is within 1.06:1 of the mockup's `#0D0D0D`,
and Crew's dark ember `#FF7A1F` measures **7.03:1** on it against the mockup's orange at 6.04:1.

So the owner compared a **light-mode screenshot of the shipped app** against a **dark-mode mockup**, and the
real difference between them is **structure**. Dark mode costs zero engineering. It has, however, **never
been rendered by any test, e2e spec, layout gate, a11y sweep or CI screenshot on either engine** — so it is
mechanically correct and visually unverified. This pass fixes that.

### The row grammar (A20.2) — one rule, three domains

**Title is always the verb.** Subtitle is the status. Trailing is a chevron.

| Row | Nothing yet | In progress | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workout** | `Push day · 5 exercises` | `Push day · 8 of 15 sets` | `Push day · 15 sets · 44 min` |
| **Cardio** | `Not logged today` | — | `25 min · 2.1 km` |
| **Meal** | `Nothing logged today` | — | `2 meals` |

**The workout subtitle names the workout only when the nav title does not already.** On a workout day the
title is `"Push day"`, so the subtitle drops the name and reads `"5 exercises"` → `"8 of 15 sets"`. On
`allDone` the title is `"Done for today"`, so the subtitle carries `"Push day · 15 sets · 44 min"`. On a rest
day it reads `"Nothing planned — bonus anytime"`. **No branch ever prints a zero** (A8).

## §7 · RISKS, STATED PLAINLY

- **RISK-1 — the keyboard fix is a hypothesis.** The three `Birth year` failures cannot be reproduced on this
  machine: there is no Mac, no Xcode, no simulator. `swift-xref` label-checks call shapes and `doctrine-lint`
  checks structure; **neither compiles SwiftUI**, and the last two reds on master were both invisible to them
  (one a scope resolution, one an `[Int]`→`Set<Int>` conversion). T-A3 is the highest-probability fix and it is
  independently justified on UX grounds — a Done bar should not appear over fields whose keyboard already has a
  return key. **CI is the adjudicator.** If it stays red, the next move is to read the xcresult, not to guess again.
- **RISK-2 — the merge deletes Home's single ink-filled primary from the card.** `VectorRow`'s shipped reasoning
  is that three logging controls are legal precisely *because* they are outline peers beneath one filled primary.
  `.crewBottomBar` is what restores it: the day's primary lives in the bar, the rows stay outline peers.
- **RISK-3 — the four sync bugs are independent of the redesign and will be blamed on it** if they ship together.
  This is exactly why ruling #4 splits the builds. Do not merge them.
- **RISK-4 — dark mode is mechanically correct and visually unverified.** T-D2 is not optional polish; it is the
  first time anyone will have seen the theme the owner says he wants.
- **RISK-5 — an element budget is the wrong target.** A18.3 records the opposite diagnosis in writing ("this was
  never too much whitespace, it was too few content elements"), and the repo's only layout gate fails a screen for
  too *large* a gap. The target is **treatment count and a stable grid**, not fewer things.
- **RISK-6 — `HomeStatesTests` is both the acceptance gate and the only way anyone sees the result**, and it is
  currently half red. Every copy string in the redesign lands there as a literal.
- **RISK-7 — the owner may not be looking at current code.** Build 121 was dispatched manually off a red CI, and
  A18 shipped three days ago. Part of what he photographed may already be answered.
