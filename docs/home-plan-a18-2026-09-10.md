# Crew — the Home screen plan II (A18), 2026-09-10

Owner-directed, from the shipped rest-day Home on the phone **after** the A17 pass landed (commit `5ed403b`).
The complaint, verbatim:

> "This is the home screen I need this improved looking at this I have questions and the next action isn't clear
> like what is the 1/3? Why is their a big blank pace? Why does the card saying rest day have a post meal CTA?
> why are there three strange divs at the bottom with workout, cardio, and meals? It doesn't make sense this
> needs work."

A17 answered "I don't know what the colours are for" by giving the week strip a sentence. It did not answer
**"what do I do now"**, and it introduced the void the owner is now looking at. This document is the contract for
A18.

**Evidence.** A 45-agent review: 7 repo lenses + 6 outside-research lenses (≥8 web searches and ≥6 fetched pages
each), 124 findings, 10 load-bearing claims put through 3 adversarial verifiers apiece, then a synthesis and a
completeness critic that re-opened the files. ~4.3M tokens.

**Two honesty notes, stated once.**

1. **The adversarial pass refuted 10 of 10 load-bearing claims.** The verifiers were instructed to default to
   `refuted` when they could not confirm, and 2-of-3 refutes killed a claim — so that number is a property of the
   harness, not a verdict on the findings. Every fact this plan acts on was therefore re-read from source by hand
   (the file:line citations below were opened, not inherited). Where a claim survives only on an agent's word it is
   marked **UNVERIFIED** and nothing is built on it.
2. **Every iOS Home file is `WRITTEN — UNVERIFIED (needs Mac)`.** There is no Xcode on this machine, there are no
   layout snapshots, and CI's simulator picker takes the newest and largest iPhone while the only automated layout
   gate runs at 375×667 — the one size where the void does not occur. §8 fixes that; until it runs, every pt figure
   here is computed from tokens, not observed.

## 0a. A18 — the Decision Registry entry (owner-authorised 2026-09-10)

Drafted here; inserted into `docs/crew-mvp-spec.md` Appendix A by **J002**.

**A18.1 — EVERY NUMERAL ON HOME IS NAMED WHERE IT SITS.** A17.1 diagnosed the disease correctly ("the flame, the
ring, the week strip and the crew strip each build a complete English sentence and render it ONLY to VoiceOver, so
a sighted user gets a glyph, two bare numerals and seven dots") and then treated only the strip and the crew
avatar. **The two bare numerals were the flame and the ring, and neither got a word.** They also sit on one row and
both read "1" on the owner's screen while meaning unrelated things — consecutive days with any post, and workouts
done this ISO week. Home gains two `secondaryText` ink captions: **"day streak"** under the flame and **"workouts
this week"** under the ring. A17.1's three hard limits are carried verbatim: secondaryText ink and never `#B84D00`
(law ③), never tappable (law ①), bridge-gated. **A8 branch:** the flame's caption renders only above a streak of
zero (and reads "streak paused" while paused); the ring's caption cannot render below one done workout because
A18.2 removes the ring there.

**A18.2 — THE RING IS A REWARD, SO IT APPEARS WHEN THERE IS ONE.** The ring renders only once the week holds at
least one completed planned workout. Today it is gated on `ringPlanned > 0, !isBridge`, so on every non-bridge
**Monday** it prints "0/3" — the identical construction A14 removed from the bridge for being "a zero used as a
verdict (A8)", one day in seven, for every user, for the life of the app. The same removal is already ratified
twice elsewhere (the bridge ring, and A5's crew pulse, which "never reads 0/n"). While paused it does not render
either (A18.6). The week's shape is still fully readable: the strip now marks every planned day (A18.7) and the
sentence still says "nothing logged yet".

**A18.3 — THE NEXT SESSION IS A BLOCK, NOT A CARD FOOTNOTE, ON THE IDLE STATES.** On rest, all-done and paused the
what's-next fact moves out of the card and becomes its own group in the space above it, with a caption heading and
an ink detail line. A3's "what's-next line on every non-bridge state" is **satisfied, not overturned** — the line
survives and moves. A17.2 recorded the correct diagnosis of the void and then shipped nothing that filled it: the
two facts it named are the miss (which is in the header group, *above* the spacer) and the shield (gated on a
perfect week, so a one-day streak never holds one). This is the fact Home already computes, renders as the card's
quietest caption, and that a rest day actually asks about. It renders **only on the idle states** (D10): on a
workout day the card already is what's next, and that state is the tallest.

**A18.4 — THE REST-DAY CARD STATES THE PREMISE, NOT ONLY THE REWARD.** "Post anything today and your 1-day streak
holds" states what you get and never states **why a rest day is a day with a requirement at all**. Crew's streak is
daily and has no training-day exemption (`streak.vectors.json` V04 is titled "rest day, silence → streak 0 at
3 AM"). The line becomes "Rest days count too — post anything and your N-day streak holds." A17.1's hard limit
bans the **consequence** ("no countdown, no risk notification, no time-pressure line"); a statement of the premise
is none of the three, and `spec:452` is untouched.

**A18.5 — THE THREE VECTORS BECOME THREE FULL-WIDTH VERB ROWS.** A14's three equal-width bordered cells are, by
Apple's own definition, the silhouette of a **segmented control** ("a linear set of two or more segments, each of
which functions as a mutually exclusive button… all segments are equal in width"). Their grammar is a stat readout
— a **noun** title over a value — and 6.6 requires verb-first control labels; the shipped VoiceOver name is
literally "Workout, Done today" on a Button. The row becomes three full-width rows: an ink verb leading ("Log
workout" · "Log cardio" · "Log a meal"), today's status trailing when there is one. **A14's ratified content is
untouched** — the same three vectors, the same equal position, the same peer weight, the same single ink-filled
primary elsewhere; only the geometry and the grammar move. It also retires the `ViewThatFits` fallback that exists
solely because three-across truncates at accessibility sizes (`VectorRow.swift:23-26`).

**A18.6 — THE PAUSED SCREEN STOPS CONTRADICTING ITSELF, AND OFFERS THE WAY OUT.** Four changes, one state:
(a) the week marks become pause-aware — a planned day inside an active pause window is never `missed`, because
Flow 7 and `spec:460` promise "pauses without penalty" and A17.1 turned those grey dots into the English sentence
"This week: Mon missed"; (b) the ring does not render while paused; (c) the card gains **"End the pause now"** —
the identical string `PauseScreen.swift:22` and `SettingsView.tsx:26` already use, so no third wording enters the
app — which is the only candidate control on any Home card that is plan-level rather than a fourth route to a
logging screen; (d) the **web pause guard** lands at the model, mirroring `HomeModel.swift:76`. Flow 7 and S07 gave
the paused card zero controls; this entry is what changes that.

**A18.7 — EVERY PLANNED DAY GETS A MARK.** A17.4(b) marked only the *next* training day, so every planned day
after it rendered byte-identically to a rest day and was named in no sentence: on a four-day plan the ring says
"of 4" while the strip can account for at most three of them, which means the strip cannot be used to read the
ring. Now that A18.1 puts the word "workouts" beside the ring, that mismatch becomes a visible contradiction.
Every planned day carries the planned mark; the **next** one stays distinguishable (filled vs hollow). A17.4(b) is
overturned and this entry says so.

**A18.8 — THE BRIDGE CARRIES ONE CTA, INCLUDING WHEN A SESSION IS OPEN.** The bridge survives until the first
**post** exists, and starting a workout creates a session, not a post — so starting and abandoning one puts the
bridge card's CTA and a "Resume workout" banner on screen together, on the one screen §1D says carries nothing
else. `OfflineSessionTests.swift:66-68` asserts **both at once**, so the rule is currently broken by a passing
test. The banner is suppressed on the bridge and the bridge's own single button reads "Resume your first workout"
when a session is open. S07's "Resume banner when session open" and §1D's "zero competing prompts" are reconciled
here, in §1D's favour, on the bridge only.

**A18.9 — THE ALL-DONE CARD REPORTS THE DAY AND POINTS FORWARD.** It is the one state with no filled control at
all, so the day you did everything right is the day the screen looks least finished. It gains today's completed
work as a summary line — built by the existing `SessionSummaryLine` twin, the same sentence the journal prints —
and loses its outline "Post a meal" (the meal row and the toolbar camera both remain). No button: the day is
closed.

**A18.10 — ONE DESTINATION, ONE ROUTE PER WEIGHT.** The toolbar camera is dropped on any state whose **card**
already offers a meal CTA. A3 ratified "a camera toolbar button on every non-bridge state"; that is **narrowed**,
the same shape of carve-out A17.3 already made to A3's enumeration. Apple's own navigation guidance names this
exact pattern — one screen carrying several routes to one action — as a cause of confusion (WWDC22 s10001). The
screen's only unlabelled control therefore disappears from the state the owner photographed.

**A18.11 — `controlOutline` IS A TOKEN OF ITS OWN.** Every outline control in the app draws its boundary with
`secondaryButtonOutline` = `hairline` = `#E9E4DD`, which measures **1.26:1 on a card and 1.19:1 on the canvas**
against 6.5's 3:1 non-text gate — `VectorRow.swift:78-79` concedes it in a comment and `contrast.test.ts:126`
exempts it by name. This is the repair `debt.md` already prescribes, taken now rather than owed: a distinct
`controlOutline` token clearing 3:1 against **both** canvas and card in both themes, applied to every outline
control, with the `contrast.test.ts` exemption deleted. `hairline` keeps its own value for real hairlines
(dividers, card edges) — a boundary between two surfaces is not a control.

**A18.12 — HOME'S OFFLINE STATE IS REACHABLE.** `HomeScreen` declares `.offline` and `load()` can only ever assign
`.failed`, `.ready` or `.empty`, so its `OfflineBanner` has never rendered. `CrewScreen` does reach it
(`CrewScreen.swift:26`); Home, Plan and Progress do not. `SyncQueue.processNext` already distinguishes `.offline`
from a failure (E6: "no network is not a failed attempt"), so the signal exists and is simply not published. It is
published, Home reads it, and `ShellStatesTests` stops asserting that a five-element literal has five elements.

**A18.13 — THE STREAK'S CADENCE IS RECORDED, NOT CHANGED.** Crew's streak is daily while its plan is weekly, which
is the root reason a rest day has to be bought with an unrelated post. The market went the other way (Hevy: "rest
days do not break your weekly streak"; Nike Run Club counts consecutive weeks), and a 60,000-post study
(BJHP/UCL/Loughborough) reports streak users describing "guilt, irritation and shame". **The cadence stays daily.**
This clause exists so the next session does not re-derive the argument, and to record that **A18.4's premise line
exists only because the streak is daily**: if the cadence ever moves to weeks, that line is deleted in the same
commit.

**UNTOUCHED BY A18:** the gamification numbers, every rejected list (Flows 4, Part II, Part IV), Ember laws ① ② ③
④ ⑤ ⑥ (no law changes; no second hue; the `controlOutline` value is a 70%-Canvas structure colour, not a control
fill — every control stays ink), the performance budgets, `minimumAgeYears`, the Concrete Doctrine C1–C14, and
every append-only vector.

**A18 ADDS NO VECTOR, AND HERE IS WHY, EXPLICITLY.** The vector suite is the *gamification* fixture contract —
every file feeds `initialState` + `events` into the streak/XP/shield engine. A18 changes no rule that engine runs:
the pause-aware week marks (A18.6a) are a **reporting** function (`weekMarks` / `weeklyRing`), they award nothing,
and V19–V23 already pin the actual pause behaviour (V20 expects `[]` for a workout completed inside a pause
window — a paused bonus pays **zero**, not +25, and the comment at `HomeModel.swift:74-75` claiming otherwise is
corrected by J030). Inventing a new fixture kind for a view-facts function would be a new abstraction for one
caller, which C1–C5 forbid. The same ruling A17 made ("a pure formatting twin ships with unit tests and no
vector") applies, and both engines get twin unit tests instead. **V57–V64 stay reserved for A16 (nutrition)** as
the ratified 2026-09-09 entry names them; the next free id after this pass is still V57.

## 2. The twelve pure defects (no decision — they are simply wrong)

Numbered as they are fixed below.

1. **J021 — the web pause guard never shipped.** `today-state.ts:100` computes `workout` with no pause term, so
   `quickCompleteAvailable` stays true and the Workout route points at `/session/new`, which re-derives
   `plannedToday` from the same pause-blind rotation and stamps `isPlannedDay: true`. iOS closed this at
   `HomeModel.swift:76` and wrote down why ("a guard per call site does not — that is exactly how F14 came back");
   the web twin was never given the same line. XP does not leak (V20 returns `[]` while paused) — what is wrong is
   the controls offered and the session stamp.
2. **J022 — a bonus workout on a non-training day marks the week differently on the two engines.** iOS guards on
   `trainingWeekdays` *before* reading any session (`HomeModel+Facts.swift:53`) and returns `.rest`; web's
   `projectWeek` tests `completedKindByDay` *first* and emits `done`. Same user, same week, two answers — and two
   different sentences, because both feed the same `WeekSummary` twin. iOS's rule wins: the ring counts planned
   days only, so a strip that marks an unplanned completion cannot be used to read the ring (A18.7's whole point).
   The bonus remains visible in the journal and on Progress.
3. **J023 — web sends the Workout row to `/plan` on a paused day.** `page.tsx:29`'s `bonusKindFor` returns null
   unless the state is rest or allDone, so paused falls through to `/plan` — a destination the file's own comment
   already calls wrong. iOS opens the bonus sheet.
4. **J024 — web's streak flame hangs its accessible name on a bare `<div>`.** `StreakFlame.tsx:5` — ARIA
   prohibits naming a generic element, which is the exact defect H007 fixed for the crew strip and which
   `page.tsx:47-48` documents in writing. `WeeklyRing.tsx:10` already does it correctly with `role="img"`. The
   count is not aria-hidden either, so a screen reader can get a bare "1".
5. **J025 — the next-up line is ink on iOS and grey on web.** `NextUpLine.swift:84` uses `inkText`;
   `TodayCard.tsx:19` uses `.muted`. The day's only forward-looking fact has two different weights.
6. **J026 — the next-up line changes position between two branches of the same card.** `TodayCard.swift:57-63`
   puts it above the control when posted and below when not; `TodayCard.tsx:34-35` reproduces it. This is the
   defect class A14 was created to remove (F10: "an outline button whose position MOVED between states"). A18.3
   dissolves it by moving the line out of the card entirely.
7. **J027 — ring geometry diverges between engines by a third.** iOS: 64 pt diameter, 8 pt stroke (1:8). Web:
   `geometry.ts:6-8` gives radius 42 and stroke 8 in a 100-unit viewBox rendered into 64 px (1:11.5), with the
   label at ~14 px against iOS's 17 pt `.headline`. No screenshot comparison of Home's header can ever agree.
8. **J028 — `today-state.ts` has zero unit tests.** Nothing under `web/tests/` imports `today-state` or
   `homeFacts`. That file is the entire web Home fact engine — today state, ring counts, vector slots, next-up
   line, quick-complete gating — and every change in this plan edits it. iOS pins the same facts with three test
   files. The harness for a real test already exists (in-memory Mongo, real handlers, no mocks).
9. **J029 — no test asserts anything about the screen the owner photographed.** `CameraDeniedTests.swift:63-64` is
   the only test that renders a posted non-bridge Home, and it asserts only that a string is *absent*. Journey 2
   seeds a member who trains every day, so its Home is a workout day; `OfflineSessionTests` asserts the bridge.
   `HomeModelTests` asserts `model.today == .rest(posted: false)` — a model value, never a view.
10. **J030 — two false comments that will be quoted against future decisions.** `TodayCard.swift:11-12` says rest
    and all-done "carry none [no filled primary], deliberately" twelve lines above the code that renders one (the
    web twin states it correctly: "once posted"). `HomeModel.swift:74-75` says a bonus while paused "correctly
    earns the unplanned +25 (V30/V31)"; V20 expects `[]` — it earns **zero**.
11. **J031 — the A17 plan's route arithmetic is wrong, and it is now in the ratified registry.**
    `home-plan-2026-09-10.md:274-288` claims "seven → five; each destination shown at most twice" while its own
    table lists posting as reached three ways, and A17.3 (`spec:1517`) carries the framing forward.
12. **J032 — the only layout gate runs at the one viewport where the defect does not occur.**
    `home-layout.spec.ts:19` pins 375×667 and `playwright.config.ts:20-22` defines 375×812 / 768 / 1280, so
    440×956 — the largest device 6.7 names, and the class of the owner's phone — is never rendered anywhere in CI;
    and the threshold is a fraction of the viewport rather than a design-system value.
