# Crew — the UI/UX plan (2026-09-09)

Owner-directed, from the second phone review of build 0.1.0 (3). The owner's asks, verbatim in spirit: the `+ set` and
`+ warm-up` hit boxes are too small; there is no way to remove a set; it is not visually clear what the work is — every
exercise needs an image and a tap-through explaining how to do it; users must choose kg or lb (and distance units) once,
by a real prompt, changeable later in Settings; the ±-tapping for weight is painful when the target is far away — scrolling
with a bigger number would be better; Home has a lot of negative space and minimalism is being wasted; workouts, cardio and
nutrition are the three primary logging vectors; nutrition needs real macro planning and daily tracking with a fixed meal
template plus a flexible remainder, with a colour scheme; and Home needs a way to swap today's workout for a custom one
when the equipment or the day does not cooperate.

This document is the builder's contract for that work. It follows `docs/improvement-plan-2026-09-08.md` in form: decisions
first (Appendix A entries A9–A16, drafted here PENDING RATIFICATION), then the work, then the checklist. Vectors stay
append-only, gamification numbers do not move, every number enters through `shared/spec-constants.json`, and the Concrete
Doctrine C1–C14 holds.

Evidence behind every decision: a 13-agent review of the repo (Home, Session, units, exercise data, design system, spec
doctrine, web parity, nutrition) plus outside research on numeric-entry patterns, unit-preference prompting, whitespace,
and exercise media licensing. Findings are cited as **F01–F21** and options as **O1–O11** from that review.

> **Ratification, 2026-09-10.** A9–A15 are ratified as drafted and as implemented through Stage 5. A16 is ratified
> **maintenance-only** — no deficit, no deficit phase, no deficit constants, no sex field, no body stat beyond the single
> current bodyweight — **with the seven no-grade clauses**, which are now in spec:240 verbatim with their enforcement
> mechanisms named beside them. Three owner additions: **A16.a** a required methodology screen, **A16.b** the App Store
> Connect age-questionnaire re-run as a Stage 9 *entry gate* (owner task), **A16.c** an 18+ gate on the nutrition-target
> surface (the app stays 13+). A13's art is ruled: **vendor** bryllim/workout-guide, never edit on disk, hash-manifest
> test as the machine-checked ShareAlike boundary — Stage 8 still gated on the lawyer answer.

- **A9 Units are asked, split, and tagged.** `units` is replaced by two fields on the user: `weightUnit` (`lb` | `kg`) and
  `distanceUnit` (`mi` | `km`). Both are defaulted at account creation from the device's measurement system
  (`Locale.current.measurementSystem`: `.us` → lb + mi, `.uk` → kg + mi, `.metric` → kg + km) and confirmed ONCE, in
  context, on the first Session screen — a single dismissible line, one tap to accept, one to flip. The unit label beside
  every weight is permanently tappable to switch. Every logged set stores the unit it was entered in (`weightUnit` on the
  set log); a preference change is therefore purely cosmetic and NEVER reinterprets history. Plate math, PR detection and
  strength trends compare on a normalised value. Closes F01, F02, F11.
- **A10 Weight entry is a tape.** The Flow 3 "smart steppers … long-press fast-scroll" mechanic is replaced for WEIGHT by
  a horizontal snapping tape: a large ink readout above a ruler that snaps to the unit's plate increment with a haptic
  tick per notch, the readout itself tapping through to a decimal keypad, and ± retained for single-notch nudges. Reps
  keep the ± stepper. Invalid values stay unreachable by clamping on commit. Closes F06, F07 and the owner's complaint.
- **A11 A set can be removed.** A swipe-left delete on any set row, with the visible-button equivalent 6.3 requires and an
  "Undo" snackbar. Removal renumbers the remaining sets, never drops an exercise below one set, and is a new engine
  behaviour → new append-only vectors. Closes F04.
- **A12 Sets prefill from reality.** Flow 3's existing promise ("rows load your last ACTUAL performance") is implemented:
  at session creation each set seeds its weight and reps from the last completed session of that exercise. A decrement
  from the floor returns to "—". Closes F05.
- **A13 Exercises have a face and an explanation.** Each seed exercise gains an optional `mediaId` and a `muscles` array.
  Line-art frames ship bundled and tinted to ink at runtime; a Settings acknowledgements screen carries attribution. Tapping
  an exercise name opens a half-height sheet: start/end frames, tinted muscle map, setup bullets, common mistakes, and the
  last-time line. Closes F08.
- **A14 Home shows the week and the work.** Home gains a seven-day strip rendering the per-day states it already computes
  (done · missed · rest · today · upcoming) and, inside the workout card, the day's actual exercise rows (name + sets×reps,
  mobility and cardio as a tail line). Below the card, a three-slot row — Workout · Cardio · Meals — shows today's state
  for each vector. Exactly one ink-filled primary remains on screen. The bridge state is untouched (§1D) and its "0/3"
  ember ring is REMOVED. Closes F09, F12, F13.
- **A15 Today's workout can be changed.** A `Change today's workout` outline button below the card opens one sheet with
  three escape hatches: *what do you have today* (Nothing · Dumbbells · Full gym → today's workout rebuilt at that
  equipment access, same movement patterns, via the existing SwapFinder tiers), *do a different day* (the existing bonus
  list), and *build my own* (the catalog picker). A custom session fills today's ring slot and earns full planned-day
  credit, but does NOT advance the rotation pointer — the scheduled kind stays next. On completion the celebration offers
  to save it as a reusable workout.
- **A16 Nutrition becomes a planned, tracked vector.** *(§7 below — narrows Flow 4's macro rejection and amends Ember

## 1. Units — the foundation everything else sits on

**Why first:** the weight tape (§2) renders a unit, the exercise sheet (§3) shows a last-time line in a unit, and macro
targets (§7) are computed from a bodyweight in a unit. Nothing downstream is correct until this is.

**The defect being fixed (F01).** There is no conversion code anywhere in the monorepo. `units` is a display suffix.
Flipping it in Settings today leaves every stored number untouched: a 135 lb bench renders "135 kg", `PlateMath` switches
from a 45 lb bar to a 20 kg bar and prescribes physically different plates, and `PersonalRecords` fires a false "new best"
in one direction and goes permanently blind in the other. Nothing warns the user. This ships today.

### Files touched
- `shared/spec-constants.json` — `kilogramsPerPound`, `poundsPerKilogram`, weight rounding increments per unit
- `shared/scripts/generate.mjs` → regenerates `SpecConstants.swift` + `spec-constants.ts`
- `web/src/lib/documents.ts` — `UserDoc.weightUnit`, `UserDoc.distanceUnit`; `SetLog.weightUnit`
- `web/src/app/api/v1/users/me/route.ts`, `auth/register/route.ts` — accept and default the two fields
- `web/src/lib/engine/weight-units.ts` (new) — `weightIn(value, from, to)`, twin of the Swift
- `ios/Crew/Engine/WeightUnits.swift` (new) — the twin
- `ios/Crew/Storage/*` — `LocalSetLog.weightUnit`, a SwiftData migration (already owed in `debt.md`)
- `ios/Crew/Api/ApiModels.swift`, `ApiSettings.swift`, `ApiSessions.swift` — the two fields, the set-log field
- `ios/Crew/Features/Settings/SettingsScreen.swift` — two picker rows, not one
- `ios/Crew/Features/Session/UnitConfirmLine.swift` (new) — the one-time in-context confirm
- `ios/Crew/Engine/PlateMath.swift`, `PersonalRecords.swift`, `SessionSummaryLine.swift` — compare on normalised values
- web twins for every read site (~22 call sites)

### Tests added
- `tests/engine/weight-units.test.ts` + `CrewTests/WeightUnitsTests.swift` — round-trip, rounding, boundary
- `tests/api/settings-units.test.ts` — the two fields default from a supplied locale hint and persist independently
- `CrewTests/UnitMigrationTests.swift` — an existing set log without `weightUnit` backfills from the account

### Vectors affected
- **NEW** `V52` weight conversion: lb→kg→lb round-trip preserves the loadable value
- **NEW** `V53` PR detection compares normalised weights across a unit change
- No existing vector is edited (append-only).

### Migration for the existing TestFlight cohort
Every existing account is `lb` by hard-coded default. Backfill `weightUnit: "lb"`, `distanceUnit: "mi"` on every existing
user and every existing set log — for the current cohort (the owner plus test accounts) this is provably correct, since
`lb` is what every number was entered under. Then the in-context confirm shows on their next session like everyone else.

### 2.2 The weight tape (A10)

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  Set 2                    ○  │
│                              │
│         185 lb               │  ← .title, tap → decimal keypad
│    ╷ ╷ ╷ ╻ ╷ ╷ ╷             │
│  ─175─180─185─190─195─       │  ← drag, snaps to 5 lb / 2.5 kg
│                              │
│    [ − ]           [ + ]     │  ← 44 pt, one notch each
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

Built with `ScrollView(.horizontal)` + `.scrollTargetLayout()` + `.scrollPosition()` and a snapping
`ScrollTargetBehavior` — iOS 17 APIs, already the deployment target, zero third-party dependencies. C6 requires it read
plainly: the behaviour is one small struct with one arithmetic line, or it does not ship. Tick geometry uses
`@ScaledMetric`. VoiceOver gets an explicit `.accessibilityAdjustableAction` so swipe-up/down still changes the value —
without it the tape is invisible to VoiceOver, which is a release-blocking 6.5 failure.

New constants (C7 — every one of these inline is a build failure): tape range per unit, tick spacing, label cadence,
points-per-notch.

**Deliberately rejected: a wheel picker.** It cannot resize for Dynamic Type at all, its unselected rows fail text
contrast, and Voice Control cannot select an option — three direct 6.5/6.7 violations on a release-blocking gate.

### 2.3 Prefill (A12) — the fix nobody asked for that matters most

The data is already computed. `SessionModel.lastTimeLine` reads the previous completed session of the exercise and renders
`last: 8 @ 135 lb` as grey text — then throws the numbers away, and the row opens at `—`. Reaching 225 lb from empty costs
45 taps or a 5.3-second hold (F05). Seeding each set from that same lookup removes most of the weight-entry problem before
the tape is even built.

`targetWeight` is hardcoded `nil` by its only writer, so plan-carried starting weights stay out of scope for now (recorded
in `debt.md`).

### 2.4 Remove a set (A11)

Swipe-left delete on any set row, plus the visible-button equivalent 6.3 mandates, plus an Undo snackbar. Renumbering,
a server-side guard that an exercise never drops below one set, and the x/y denominator all change → new vectors:

- **NEW** `V54` removing a set renumbers the remainder and recomputes `setsPlanned`
- **NEW** `V55` an exercise cannot be reduced below one set

### 2.5 Exercise detail (A13)

Tap the exercise name → a `.medium` detent sheet. This is the SECOND occurrence of this pattern alongside
`Features/Plan/ExerciseSheet.swift` — **C5 says duplicate it, do not extract it.** Extraction waits for the third.

Content: start/end line-art frames side by side, tinted muscle map, 3–5 setup bullets, 2–3 common mistakes, the last-time
line. Instruction copy is written in-house with externally-focused cues (the motor-learning evidence for external focus is
strong, and it sidesteps every provenance question about borrowed text).

Media: bundled line-art SVGs, tinted to ink at runtime, never edited on disk. ~1–3 MB total — three orders of magnitude
under the App Store cap and far under the cellular-download prompt, and fully offline, which is the point: the gym
basement is exactly where the signal dies. Seed gains `mediaId` and `muscles`; five files change
(`exercises.json`, `check-seeds.mjs`, `render-seed.mjs`, `SeedCatalog.swift`, then `generate` + `check-drift`).

**Open legal item:** the candidate set is CC BY-SA. ShareAlike attaches to *adapted material*; bundling unmodified files
is a collection, so the app source and paid model are untouched — but this needs a 30-minute lawyer confirmation before
the assets land, and a Settings acknowledgements screen ships with them either way. Recorded as a blocker in `debt.md`.

## 3. Home (A14)

### The diagnosis
Home is 24–66% dead canvas in every state and the entire interactive surface terminates ~385 pt from the top — the whole
control set lives in the upper half of an 852 pt phone while the reachable thumb half is the empty half (F09). The root is
a top-aligned `VStack` in a `ScrollView` with no `Spacer`, no bottom anchor, and a uniform 16 pt gap between every element,
which is *why* the emptiness reads as absence rather than confidence: with no rhythm, nothing is grouped, so nothing looks
composed.

The research is blunt about the cause: this is not too much whitespace, it is too few content elements to justify the
whitespace. A flame, a ring, a card reading "5 exercises + mobility", two outline buttons and an avatar strip are mostly
chrome and symbols — almost none of it is *content*.

Home also computes three things and renders none of them (F12): `weeklyRing: [DayRingState]` resolves done/missed/rest/
today/upcoming per ISO weekday and is passed to `WeeklyRing(days:)`, **which never reads the parameter**. The user sees
"2/4" and one continuous arc and cannot tell which two days they hit.

### The layout

```
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│  🔥 12              ◔ 2/4      │
│  M   T   W   T   F   S   S     │  ← the states already computed
│  ●   ○   ●   ◑   ·   ·   ·     │
│                                │
│ ┌────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ PUSH DAY                   │ │
│ │ Bench Press          3×8   │ │  ← the actual work
│ │ Incline DB Press     3×10  │ │
│ │ Cable Fly            3×12  │ │
│ │ Overhead Press       3×8   │ │
│ │ Lateral Raise        3×15  │ │
│ │ + mobility · 3 holds       │ │
│ │ ██ Start workout ████████  │ │  ← the ONE filled primary
│ └────────────────────────────┘ │
│  [ Change today's workout ]    │
│                                │
│ ┌──────┐ ┌──────┐ ┌──────┐    │
│ │Workout││Cardio││ Meals│      │  ← three co-equal vectors
│ │  ●   ││  ○   ││ 2/3  │      │
│ └──────┘ └──────┘ └──────┘    │
└────────────────────────────────┘
```

Typography carries the hierarchy, not colour — the identity line (`PUSH DAY`) is currently `.caption` while the *count*
is `.title3`, so the size line is typographically louder than what you are actually doing. That inverts.

Spacing gains two tokens so macro and micro rhythm diverge (section gap ≥ 2× row gap); the uniform 16 pt everywhere goes.
Controls bottom-anchor into the thumb zone, which 6.7 already requires ("primary actions stay bottom-anchored" at Pro Max).

**The bridge state is untouched** — §1D forbids adding anything to it — and its ember `0/3` ring is removed, since it is
simultaneously a competing prompt on the one screen the spec says must have none, an orange element that is not a reward,
and a zero used as a verdict (F13, A8).

Also swept in this pass: the stray "Quick complete" that renders under a card saying the plan is paused (F14 — the flag is
computed before the pause branch), the CrewStrip's 40 pt avatars that look tappable and are not (F20), and the nav-bar
camera button, the only control on Home with no coded ≥44 pt guarantee (F03).

## 4. Change today's workout (A15)

One sheet, three escape hatches:

```
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│  Change today's workout        │
│                                │
│  WHAT DO YOU HAVE?             │
│  [ Nothing                   ] │  → rebuild at bodyweight access
│  [ Dumbbells                 ] │  → rebuild at dumbbell access
│  [ Full gym                  ] │
│                                │
│  OR                            │
│  [ Do a different day       › ] │  → the existing bonus list
│  [ Build my own             › ] │  → the catalog picker
└────────────────────────────────┘
```

The equipment rebuild is nearly free: `equipmentAccess` groups (`fullGym` / `dumbbells` / `bodyweight`) already exist in
the seed, `SwapFinder.swapCandidates(for:access:experience:seed:)` already filters by them in swapGroup → pattern → region
tiers, and `kind: "custom"` is already a named value in `PlanDraftWorkout`. Push day at bodyweight access becomes push-ups,
pike push-ups and dips — the same patterns, different equipment.

**Credit rules (new engine behaviour → new vectors):**
- a custom session **fills today's ring slot** and earns full planned-day credit — showing up is the whole philosophy
- it does **not** advance the rotation pointer: the pointer is derived from the last completed session whose kind is in
  the plan's cycle, and `custom` is not in the cycle, so this falls out of the existing design rather than fighting it
- **NEW** `V56` a custom session on a training day counts as planned and leaves the rotation pointer unmoved

On completion, the celebration offers "Save as a workout?" — one tap at the moment it is obviously worth keeping.

## 5. The three vectors (A14)

Today they are not close to co-equal (F10): a workout is one tap on the only 56 pt ink primary; cardio is two taps on a
44 pt outline whose position *moves between states*; a meal is a nav-bar glyph that is absent on the bridge. Logging a
45-minute walk changes Home not at all.

The three-slot row makes them peers positionally while the plan's workout keeps the single filled primary on a training
day — which is how this stays legal under "one primary action per view" (enforced by four separate rules: 6.1's Five
States Law, §1B, S07, and §1D).

Equality also has to hold beyond Home, or the row is a lie: three distinct journal row types, three marks per day in the
heat map, and equal streak weight. A standalone cardio log currently writes a post of `type: "workout"`, so every count of
"workouts" silently includes walks — that gets its own type.

**The scope warning, explicitly:** three co-equal vectors is compatible with this app's philosophy only as *ritual*
equality — same streak weight, same journal row, same Home slot. It is not magnitude equality, because Flow 4 forbids the
app from grading. §7 is where that line gets tested.

## 6. Web parity

Data and engine parity is mandatory: the units model, unit-tagged set logs, set removal, exercise metadata, macro data and
Home's content set must be identical on both platforms. Input affordances may diverge — a drag-tape belongs on a phone, a
number field belongs on a desktop — and each divergence is logged in `debt.md` in the commit that creates it.

The twin is already behind on exactly these surfaces (F19): web cannot add a set at all, its bonus workout is one
pre-chosen link rather than a chooser, and the two platforms already disagree on primary-button height. Exercise media is
taken iOS-first because web has no `public/` asset directory at all.

## 7. Nutrition (A16)

The largest amendment in this document, and the only one that touches a permanently-rejected list.

**RATIFIED 2026-09-10, MAINTENANCE-ONLY.** Targets are maintenance estimates only: **no deficit**, no deficit phase, no
deficit constants, no sex field, and no body stat beyond the single current bodyweight. A deficit, if ever wanted, is a
separate amendment with its own phase and compliance checklist — and is **not stubbed for** (preparing for it is scope
expansion, Appendix C). Stage 9 still does not begin until **A16.b** clears: the owner re-answers the App Store Connect
age questionnaire with A16 in mind and records the resulting rating in `docs/progress.md`.

**Surviving verbatim and governing everything below:**
- spec:452 — *"Weights & calories never pressure you."* This is the rule the zero-floor, the no-red rule, the no-minus rule
  and the no-notification rule all answer to.
- spec:358 (E15) — *"No effort scoring, no anomaly flags, ever."* No plausibility check on an entered macro, no "that
  seems low for a dinner", no under-reporting flag. The app counts what it is told and says nothing about it.
- Ember laws ① ③ ④ ⑤ hold **inside** the feature: no macro colour on a button, tab, toggle or link; macro colours are
  shape colours only; every numeral and every letter is ink.
- `mealXpDailyCap` stays 3, `xpMealPost` stays 15, V03 / V12 / V18b / V24 / V26 are untouched.

### 7.2 The model

- **"Your usual day"** — the fixed template. Not "Template", not "Plan" (Plan is already a tab and would collide). One to
  six slots, each a meal outline with protein/carb/fat grams. Check a slot off when you eat it.
- **"Flex"** — the flexible remainder. Full form *"Flex calories"* on first exposure and in the empty state, defined in
  place: *"Flex · 460 cal — anything you want, on top of your usual meals."* `Flex = daily target − template total`.
- **A skipped template meal never becomes Flex.** The day simply comes in lower. This is the rule that stops the feature
  from becoming a game about banking calories by skipping breakfast.
- **Flex floors at zero.** Past it, a separate line reads `240 over` — a measurement, never a verdict.
- **The framing flips at 50%.** Under half a target the bar reads *consumed* (`128 g in`); past half it reads *remaining*
  (`52 g to go`), which is the number that sizes your next meal. The fraction `128 / 180 g` is always visible either way.
- **Two objects, never one.** A photo post and a macro entry have separate lifecycles: deleting a photo never deletes
  numbers, deleting numbers never deletes a photo. E3 already demands this shape for posts vs sessions.
- **Targets are protein-first, maintenance-only.** Bodyweight + activity tier → energy; protein from bodyweight; fat at a
  floor of max(20% of energy, 0.5 g/kg); carbs as the remainder. Every number editable, the whole flow skippable to
  manual entry. No deficit is ever prescribed.

### 7.4 The palette (Ember law ⑥ amendment)

Computed and verified with CIEDE2000 and Machado et al. (2009) colour-vision simulation at full severity across normal,
protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia — not picked by eye.

| Token | Light | Dark | Contrast (bone / dark) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `macroProtein` — pine | `#2E4E28` | `#BDD9B9` | 8.86:1 / 12.04:1 |
| `macroCarbs` — slate | `#5A8EB4` | `#3D7392` | 3.32:1 / 3.55:1 |
| `macroFat` — mulberry | `#6C315F` | `#DAADDB` | 8.84:1 / 9.59:1 |
| `macroProteinTrack` | `#D9DDD4` | `#42473D` | — |
| `macroCarbsTrack` | `#E0E7EB` | `#212D33` | — |
| `macroFatTrack` | `#E3D8DD` | `#4A3C46` | — |

Worst intra-palette separation ΔE00 **23.5** light / **22.6** dark (~10× the just-noticeable difference). Minimum
separation from the reward orange: **24.8** (protein), **44.5** (carbs), **30.0** (fat) — for comparison, MyFitnessPal's
carbs orange would score 7 and Okabe-Ito's vermilion 3.7. Chroma is capped at C\* ≤ 36 against the ember's C\* 90, so the
flame remains ~2.5–3× the most saturated object in the app: that ratio is the mechanism that keeps this reading as three
inks with a tilt rather than a candy dashboard.

**Amended law ⑥:** *"The 20% stays neutral; no second hue, with one bounded exception. Three categorical macro identity
tokens — protein, carbs, fat — exist for nutrition surfaces only, bound by four hard rules: chroma never above C\* 36;
never inside CIELAB hue 20°–95° (the ember wedge, where #FF6600 sits at h52); identity only, never status, so a macro
colour never changes with over/under/on-target; and no surface renders an ember element and a macro fill together.
Semantics keep green/red/gray for their meanings, and no semantic token appears on a nutrition surface."*

**The redundant encoding is mandatory, not optional** — WCAG 1.4.1 is Level A, and Apple now surfaces "Differentiate
Without Color Alone" as App Store metadata. All five ship, and the screen must be fully correct with every macro token
forced to plain ink:

1. **Fixed order forever: Protein → Carbs → Fat.** Never reordered, never sorted by value. Position is the primary encoder.
2. **A P / C / F letter on every coloured element**, drawn in ink, never in the macro colour. This is what carries
   greyscale — and it is why light protein and light fat sharing L\*30 is an acceptable trade.
3. **Direct numeric labels on every bar**, never a colour-only legend.
4. **Fill treatment as a third channel**: protein solid, carbs solid + ink hairline, fat outline + hairline.
5. **State is never colour.** Over/under/on-target renders in ink words and ink weight. A macro colour is pure identity
   and never bends — that is precisely the bug Cronometer's own CEO has acknowledged in their product.

**Two hazards on the record.** `macroCarbs` sits only ΔE00 7.4 from the existing `success` token under deuteranopia
(blue and green converge) — they must never render on the same surface, which the amended law already forbids. And ember
`#FF6600` on bone measures **2.77:1**, below the 3:1 graphical-object gate: a pre-existing defect that becomes conspicuous
the moment three new tokens ship that all pass. Logged in `debt.md`; same-hue fixes are `#DF5908` (3.56:1) or `#D45313`
(3.93:1).

Five independently shippable phases behind one ratification (given 2026-09-10) and one entry gate (A16.b, still open).

  tokens in `design-tokens.json`; a `macros` block in `spec-constants.json` (Atwater factors, protein g/kg in tenths so
  both engines do integer arithmetic, fat floor, gram caps, the 50% flip threshold, `nutritionQuestionCount`). Pipeline
  edits to `generate.mjs`, `render-seed.mjs`, `check-seeds.mjs`, `SeedCatalog.swift`. New `check-seeds` assertion: **no
  outline may carry a quality tag or a healthiness word.** New `macro-palette.test.ts` asserting contrast ≥ 3:1 and
  ΔE00 ≥ 20 from ember under all four vision models, so the palette cannot silently drift.
- **N2 — engine twins + vectors.** `MacroMath` / `MacroDay` / `MealTemplate` on both engines. Eight vectors, V57–V64
  (V52/V53 belong to A9, V56 to A15). **V63 is the one that matters most: a macro entry awards zero XP and does not
  increment `day.meals`** — it machine-checks the boundary between the graded and ungraded halves of the app forever.
  Each new Swift test file must be added explicitly to `ios/Package.swift` or it silently never runs.
- **N3 — server.** `documents-nutrition.ts`, four routes under `api/v1/nutrition/`, export + delete-cascade coverage
  (E9), and every new route/method pair declared in `standing-registry.ts` or the generated standing check fails.
- **N4 — iOS.** `Features/Nutrition/` (~12 files, forced by the 200-line cap). `NutritionQuestionsScreen` is copied from
  `PlanQuestionsScreen` per **C5 — duplicate, do not extract.** `JournalFacts.line` stays untouched: the plate journal
  stays numberless.
- **N6 — compliance surface (A16.a + A16.c), and it ships WITH N4, not after it.** A methodology screen naming
  Mifflin-St Jeor, the activity-multiplier source, and the line that this is an estimate and a clinician should be
  consulted — every source linkable, and reachable without hunting (App Review 1.4.1's rejection language covers
  *calculations*, and a TDEE estimate is one). Plus the 18+ guard: one constant, one guard, **V65**. Under 18 the whole
  target vector — surface, Settings rows, onboarding questions — is simply absent; no separate copy, no upsell, no
  "unlock at 18" line. **GAP (open):** the account stores a birth *year*, not a birthdate, and it is optional on the
  Sign in with Apple path — so an account can carry no age at all. Conservative reading adopted: age is
  `currentYear − birthYear` (the arithmetic `requireSignupGates` already uses) and an absent year reads as under 18.
  Nothing is asked; no existing account is re-prompted.
- **N5 — web parity + e2e + a11y.** Three pages, five components, `journey5-nutrition.spec.ts` at 375/768/1280, plus a
  **manual Grayscale-filter pass on both platforms** — the palette's one accepted limitation has to be confirmed by eye.

**One addition to the T047 launch audit:** the existing gate greps the source for the rejected list including "calorie/
macro entry". After A16 that grep stops being meaningful and must be replaced by the no-grade clauses, or the launch gate
silently loses its teeth on exactly the rule this amendment exists to preserve.
